# Remaining limitations

- Hospital street address, public phone, thana, area, specialty lists, maps, and doctor affiliations are not verified. They stay empty on purpose.
- Pima redistribution and several dataset licenses remain NOT VERIFIED.
- DiaBD is not a calibrated personal probability. Cleveland is not community screening. The WHO tool is a chart lookup for ages 40 to 74.
- Article editing is not stored. Bookmarks and hospital favorites stay in the browser.
- Blood donation scheduled, completed, and expired are not stored. The network is a simulation.
- Admin approval of a registration string is not a government or BMDC verification.
- Pending appointments do not open patient records.
- Phone-width layout and the four signed-in demo clicks were not repeated in the browser in this pass.
- No public deployment was made.
