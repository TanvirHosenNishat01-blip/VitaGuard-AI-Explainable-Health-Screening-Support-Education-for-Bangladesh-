# Business value and commercial viability

**Component:** 3 of 5, 20 points  
**Governance:** NOT SUBMITTED — NOT APPROVED — NO RECRUITMENT AUTHORIZED

This note separates a possible future business from the product that exists today. No revenue, price, market size, customer count, or cost saving is stated, because none has been measured.

## Value proposition today

The working value is clarity. A patient, a doctor, and an administrator can see which screening tool is in use, what it refuses to do, and who is allowed to open a chart. That reduces a specific kind of confusion: treating four different methods as one diagnosis.

It does not currently reduce hospital cost, replace a clinician, or process a paid workflow.

## Who it is for, now and later

| Now | Later, only after approval |
| --- | --- |
| A competition jury and a local technical demo | A clinic or teaching program that wants a bounded education workspace |
| A developer maintaining the local app | An institution that licenses the software for its own staff training |

No healthcare organization is a customer of this repository.

## Proposed business model

A future model could be a paid institutional license for the education and workflow layer, with screening models remaining subject to their own licenses. A subscription for individual patients is a poor fit while the product cannot claim clinical use.

This is a proposal. There is no price list, contract, or pilot.

## Commercialization roadmap

1. Keep the local demo honest and non-clinical.
2. Confirm dataset licenses before any redistribution.
3. Obtain the regulatory and ethics permissions that are currently absent.
4. Only then discuss a paid pilot with an institution.
5. Price the work after an infrastructure and support cost study. That study has not been done.

- The collaboration notes in `PHASE06C1_INSTITUTIONAL_COLLABORATION_FRAMEWORK.md` describe partnerships that would be required later. They also forbid claiming those partnerships now.

## Risks

A judge who hears a revenue story will be hearing something this project does not have. The credible commercial statement is that the governance and explanation layer is the part an institution might someday license, and that clinical use is blocked until independent review exists.
