## 3. Granular Institutional Profiles & Operational Requirements

### Category 1: Upazila Health Complexes (UHCs) — Primary Care Level
- **Why Collaboration is Critical:** UHCs represent the frontline of the Bangladesh public health system, serving rural upazila populations. Validating DiaBD at the UHC level tests whether the model functions where healthcare resources are most constrained.
- **Candidate Example Zones (Proposed):**
  - Mithapukur or Pirganj Upazila Health Complex (Rangpur Division).
  - Paba or Godagari Upazila Health Complex (Rajshahi Division).
  - Golapganj or Beanibazar Upazila Health Complex (Sylhet Division).
- **Publicly Verified Facts:** UHCs operate under the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MOHFW). They typically have 50 beds, outpatient consultation rooms, basic laboratories (semi-automated biochemistry), and are led by an Upazila Health & Family Planning Officer (UH&FPO).
- **Required Institutional Permissions:**
  1. Formal administrative approval from DGHS, MOHFW (Management Information System - MIS wing).
  2. Written concurrence from the Civil Surgeon of the respective district.
  3. Operational clearance from the UH&FPO.
- **Data Governance Agreements:** Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Institutional Data Use Agreement (DUA) co-signed by DGHS representatives.
- **Recruitment Feasibility:** Daily general outpatient attendance is typically $300\text{--}500$ patients/day. Recruiting 10–15 eligible consented participants/day per site is operationally feasible.
- **Laboratory Capability Requirements:** Must verify on-site availability of functioning centrifuges, cold-chain refrigeration ($2\text{--}8^\circ\text{C}$), and reliable backup power (IPS/generator). If local enzymatic testing is unavailable, daily cold-box courier transport of plasma tubes to the district hospital is required.

---

### Category 2: District Sadar Hospitals — Secondary Care Level
- **Why Collaboration is Critical:** Captures mixed urban-rural populations presenting to secondary referral facilities, providing larger clinical throughput and dedicated laboratory staff.
- **Candidate Example Zones (Proposed):**
  - 250-Bed District Sadar Hospital, Pabna.
  - 250-Bed District Sadar Hospital, Bogura.
  - 250-Bed District Sadar Hospital, Dinajpur.
- **Publicly Verified Facts:** District Sadar hospitals have full pathology departments, automated biochemistry analyzers, and resident medical officers. Led by a Hospital Superintendent or Director.
- **Required Institutional Permissions:**
  1. Approval from the Hospital Superintendent.
  2. Concurrence from District Civil Surgeon and DGHS.
- **Laboratory Capability Requirements:** Automated clinical chemistry analyzer capable of running enzymatic hexokinase or glucose oxidase reagents with daily calibrators and controls.

---

### Category 3: Diabetic Association of Bangladesh (BADAS) Affiliated Centers
- **Why Collaboration is Critical:** BADAS operates the world's largest non-profit diabetes healthcare delivery network. Their affiliated district diabetes associations (e.g., in Sylhet, Rajshahi, Chattogram) conduct high-volume screening with standardized diagnostic pathways.
- **Candidate Example Zones (Proposed):**
  - Sylhet Diabetic Association Hospital.
  - Rajshahi Diabetic Association Hospital.
  - Chattogram Diabetic Association Hospital.
- **Publicly Verified Facts:** BADAS affiliated hospitals maintain dedicated diabetes registries, certified diabetes educators, and standardized laboratory blood collection protocols.
- **Required Institutional Permissions:**
  1. Formal research partnership approval from BADAS National Council / Research Division.
  2. Approval from local Diabetic Association Executive Committee and Hospital Director.
- **Laboratory Capability Requirements:** Excellent. In-house HPLC HbA1c analyzers and automated clinical chemistry systems with established internal quality control.

---

### Category 4: Accredited Reference Laboratories & Academic Centers
- **Why Collaboration is Critical:** An independent reference laboratory is essential to perform monthly blind split-sample cross-validation (5% of specimens) to ensure external test accuracy.
- **Candidate Candidate Institutions (Proposed):**
  - International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (icddr,b) Diagnostic Laboratories.
  - National Institute of Preventive and Social Medicine (NIPSOM).
  - BRAC James P Grant School of Public Health (JPGSPH).
- **Publicly Verified Facts:** icddr,b Diagnostic Services holds international ISO 15189 accreditation for medical testing laboratories, providing gold-standard verification for venous glucose and HPLC HbA1c.
- **Required Institutional Permissions:**
  1. Formal Collaborative Research Agreement (CRA).
  2. Material Transfer Agreement (MTA) for blood plasma specimens.
  3. Approval from Institutional Review Board (IRB) / Ethics Committee.
