### 3.4 Missing Central Laboratory Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- A formal, legally binding institutional agreement must be executed with an accredited clinical laboratory (e.g., icddr,b Diagnostic Services or BIRDEM Central Laboratory).
- The SLA must explicitly guarantee:
  1. Adherence to SOP-04 (pre-analytical glycolysis prevention and rapid plasma processing).
  2. Adherence to SOP-05 (NGSP-certified HPLC analysis with $<2\%$ analytical CV).
  3. Performance of blinded duplicate testing on a random $5\%$ aliquot subset for quality audit.
  4. Secure, pseudonymized electronic result transmission compliant with SOP-08.

- **Empirical Pilot Calibration Check:** While $\text{Var}(\text{LP}) = 0.684\text{--}0.689$ has been mathematically verified from the sealed DiaBD test set, the lead biostatistician must review whether an initial 100-patient pilot cohort should be evaluated to verify the empirical variance in a true prospective primary care population prior to locking the final sample size.
- **Intra-Cluster Correlation ($\text{ICC}$):** The clustering design effect ($\text{DEFF} = 1.30$) assumes $\text{ICC} = 0.0005$ across the 6 regional hubs. This assumption must be formally documented in the signed Statistical Analysis Plan.
